# 📊 NEPSE Daily Intelligence

> Personalized daily market intelligence for Nepal's stock market — built around evidence, probability, and disciplined one-week swing-trade thinking.

## 🎯 Purpose

This repository is the long-term memory and research log for the daily NEPSE briefing. It is designed to answer four questions every trading day:

1. **What happened?** — NEPSE, turnover, breadth, sectors and major market-moving events.
2. **What matters?** — the signals that can actually change the next few sessions.
3. **What may happen next?** — base, bull and bear scenarios with probabilities.
4. **What did we get right or wrong?** — previous trade ideas are reviewed against actual outcomes.

## 🧠 Trading philosophy

- No certainty claims; use probability and scenarios.
- Prefer setups with clear invalidation over exciting stories.
- Separate **facts**, **interpretation**, and **forecast**.
- Track prediction quality over time instead of selectively remembering winners.
- A trade idea without a risk level is incomplete.
- One-week setups are tactical ideas, not long-term investment recommendations.

## 📈 Setup scoring

Each setup should consider:

- Trend / price structure
- Volume and liquidity
- Relative sector strength
- Momentum
- Catalyst / news
- Risk-to-reward
- Market regime
- Failure conditions

**Confidence is not probability of profit.** It is an assessment of how strong the evidence is for the stated setup.

## ⚠️ Risk note

This is research and decision-support content, not guaranteed financial advice. NEPSE can move sharply and liquidity can be uneven. Always verify live prices, disclosures and your own risk limits before trading.
